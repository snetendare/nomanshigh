WIN: each colour has a stash and, on each lane, a T1 tower in front of a T2. Kill the T1 to expose the T2 behind it. The stash is shielded until all of that colour's towers are down. A busted stash leaves that player on a last life: kill them too. Last one alive wins. Once every stash is gone a ring closes on the middle.
COINS: last hits pay coins (so do towers, narcs and bounties). Spend them at your stash on minions, sent at a rival or to the lab, and on substances.
LEVELS: +6% damage and +8 HP each, shown on everyone's name. Higher level = bigger bounty. Three levels ahead of the lowest and you get a crown and show on every map.
THE LAB: be the only colour in the middle circle (minions count) to capture it: a batch on capture, then coins for as long as you stand in it.
RANGE: towers and stashes only take damage from inside their ring, and towers shoot players inside it.
RAMPS: launch ramps, bounce pads and speed gates switch on for 20 s every 4 minutes (and all through warmup).
COMEBACK: you level faster the further you are behind the top level (+30% XP per level, plus a slow free trickle), and the leader levels slower. You can shop while dead.
AURAS: everyone can see what you are on: one dot circles your head per active substance, in its slot colour (cyan G-Fuel, pink Shrooms, green Copium, brown Brownie, blue Water). The same dots show in the player list.
MODES: the host picks 1v1v1 (triangle, a lane between every pair) or 1v1 (two lanes around the lab). Joiners get whatever the host picked.
Empty colours are played by bots. Desktop + mouse only.
